Goat Soup

Welcome viewers to this blog in which I will talk about how to create Goat Soup. By the end of this blog you will be a professional and include your own spin to the recipes.

Ingredients

  • 1/2 Cup of God
  • 1/2 Cup of Family
  • 1 Cup of Air Fryer
  • 2 Jar of 2016 Memories
  • 1 Teaspoon of Lebron

Instructions

  1. Get a pot of water and boil it for 5 mins.
  2. Dump all the ingredients into the boiling pot.
  3. Stur thoroughly until the texture is soft.
  4. Turn off the fire and enjoy the Goat Soup.
